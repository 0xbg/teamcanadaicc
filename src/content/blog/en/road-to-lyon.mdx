# The Road to Lyon: Team Canada Returns to the ICC

After a ten-year absence, Team Canada is preparing to return to the **International Catering Cup (ICC)** — one of the most prestigious culinary competitions in the world, held in Lyon, France.

## A Decade in the Making

The last time Canada competed at the ICC was over a decade ago. Since then, the Canadian culinary scene has exploded with talent, innovation, and recognition on the global stage. It{`'`}s time to bring that energy back to Lyon.

> "This is not just about winning a trophy. It's about showing the world what Canadian cuisine can be — bold, diverse, and rooted in our incredible local ingredients."
> — Chef Kevin

## The Journey Ahead

The road to Lyon is paved with rigorous training, recipe development, and countless hours in the kitchen. Our team of three chefs, supported by Bar George and a growing family of partners, is working tirelessly to prepare a competition program that will make Canada proud.

### Training Milestones

1. **June 2026** — Team selection and initial concept development
2. **August 2026** — Intensive kitchen training begins in Montreal
3. **October 2026** — Recipe finalization and presentation practice
4. **January 2027** — Full competition simulation and final adjustments

## How You Can Help

Bringing Canada back to the world stage requires more than talent — it takes resources. From premium ingredients to travel logistics, every contribution brings us one step closer to Lyon.
