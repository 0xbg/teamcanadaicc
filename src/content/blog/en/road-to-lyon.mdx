After a ten-year absence, Team Canada is preparing to return to the **International Catering Cup (ICC)** — one of the most prestigious culinary competitions in the world, held in Lyon, France, alongside the Bocuse d'Or at Sirha.

## A Decade in the Making

The last time Canada competed at the ICC was in 2017. Since then, the Canadian culinary scene has exploded with talent, innovation, and recognition on the global stage. It{`'`}s time to bring that energy back to Lyon.

> "This is not just about winning a trophy. It's about showing the world what Canadian cuisine can be — bold, diverse, and rooted in our incredible local ingredients."
> — Chef Kevin Ramasawmy, Team Coach

## The Journey Ahead

The road to Lyon is paved with rigorous training, recipe development, and countless hours in the kitchen. Our team of three chefs, supported by Bar George, is working tirelessly to prepare a competition program that will make Canada proud.

### Training Milestones

1. **June 2026** — Team selection and initial concept development
2. **August 2026** — Intensive kitchen training begins in Montréal
3. **October 2026** — Recipe finalization and presentation practice
4. **January 2027** — Full competition simulation and final adjustments
