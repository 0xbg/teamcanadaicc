About the Event

The International Catering Cup

Held in Lyon during Sirha, alongside the Bocuse d'Or, the International Catering Cup brings together national teams for one of the hospitality industry's most respected culinary competitions.

It places creativity, technical precision, service, pastry and teamwork on an international stage before leading professionals, media and decision-makers from across the global food and hospitality sector.

Presented by Team Canada

Team Canada will be represented by Chef de Cuisine and Team Captain Donnel English, Pastry Chef Nicolas Parsy, and Executive Chef and Team Coach Kevin Ramasawmy.

All three are part of the culinary team at Bar George in Montréal, giving the campaign a unified identity, a credible home base and a strong foundation for training, storytelling, media and sponsor activations.

A 10-Year Comeback Story

A National Culinary Comeback

Canada has not competed at the International Catering Cup since 2017. ICC 2027 gives partners a rare opportunity to help bring the country back to the culinary world stage, and to be part of the journey from preparation in Montréal to competition day in Lyon.

01

The Road to Lyon

The campaign follows the chefs as they train, develop their competition program and prepare to represent Canada. Partners can be integrated naturally through products, services, experiences and shared storytelling.

02

Content Across the Campaign

Chef-led content, behind-the-scenes moments, product integration, social media recognition, professional photography and coverage surrounding the competition.

03

A Story That Continues

The campaign does not end on competition day. Results coverage, behind-the-scenes content, sponsor recaps and post-event activations extend the story beyond Lyon.

Meet the Team

Three Chefs. One Kitchen. One Mission.

Drawn from the brigade at Bar George in Montréal, Team Canada combines leadership, pastry expertise and executive experience.

Team Captain

Donnel English

Chef de Cuisine, Bar George

Chef Donnel English's culinary story begins in Grenada, where his grandparents ran a farm and produce shop. After arriving in Montréal in 2007, his potential was quickly recognized, leading him to train at the Pearson School of Culinary Arts.

His career has taken him through the Ritz-Carlton, Maison Boulud, and New York's NoMad. He returned to Montréal to join Chef Kevin Ramasawmy at Bar George, progressing from Sous Chef to Chef de Cuisine.

Career Highlights: Team Captain, ICC 2027 · Chef de Cuisine, Bar George · Ritz-Carlton · Maison Boulud · NoMad, New York

Pastry Chef

Nicolas Parsy

Pastry Chef, Bar George

Raised in Pas-de-Calais in northern France, Nicolas discovered the power of food watching his mother and Polish grandmother bring family and friends together around the table. He developed his craft in France and Switzerland before moving to Montréal in 2018.

Nicolas approaches pastry through a modern interpretation of French tradition — flavour first, supported by precise technique, contrasting textures and thoughtful presentation.

Career Highlights: 2025 National Pastry Chef, Société des chefs du Québec · 2nd Place, Roland Mesnier North America Pastry Trophy · One to Watch Award, Canada's 100 Best

Team Coach

Kevin Ramasawmy

Executive Chef, Bar George

Kevin's connection to food began in Mauritius, cooking with his mother. He moved to Montréal in 2004 at age 19. His career took him to Canoe in Toronto, Splendido, Daniel in New York, and Maison Boulud at the Ritz-Carlton.

In 2017, he opened Bar George at Le Mount Stephen. He has cooked for the TGR Haas F1 Team during the Canadian Grand Prix and represented Bar George internationally. As Team Coach, he brings international kitchen experience and operational leadership.

Career Highlights: Executive Chef, Bar George · Canoe · Splendido · Daniel · Maison Boulud · TGR Haas F1 Team · Canadian Grand Prix
